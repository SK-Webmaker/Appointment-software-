## Posting notes

- **Order:** reel first (reach), carousel 2–3 days later, then pricing. People who see the reel and check the profile land on the prices.
- **Pin** the reel and the pricing post to the top of the profile grid.
- **Reel cover:** upload `websites-reel-cover.jpg` as the cover so the grid shows the hook.
- **Audio:** the reel's soundtrack is original, so it's safe to post anywhere, including business accounts. On TikTok you can also add a trending sound at low volume underneath it.
- **Replying to "DEMO" DMs:** ask for the business name, their Instagram or current site, and 2–3 sites they like the look of. That's enough to build the demo.
