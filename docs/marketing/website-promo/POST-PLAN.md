# Website promo: post plan

Goal: get the reel, carousel and pricing post out fast, in the slots that get the most views, with every "DEMO" enquiry answered automatically. Everything here is free. Times are Melbourne (AEDT).

**Why these slots**

- **Days and times:** Australia's peak is Mon–Thu, 5–10pm, and Thursday is the strongest day. Saturday is the weakest. Sunday has a short 8–9pm window. *(Sprout Social, 2026 AU data)*
- **Reels:** they do best in the evening. Instagram's biggest ranking signal in 2026 is "sends" (people DM-ing your post to a friend), so every caption asks people to send it on. *(Buffer, 9.6M posts)*
- **Salon owners:** most salons close on Monday, so Sunday night and Monday are when they actually scroll.

## Right after posting: set up the free DEMO auto-replies (~10 min)

In **Meta Business Suite → Inbox → Automations** (free, covers Instagram and Facebook):

1. **Custom keywords:** keyword `DEMO`, for Instagram and Messenger. Reply:
   > Hey! 👋 Thanks for asking about a free demo. To build yours, just send us:
   > 1. your business name and what you do
   > 2. your Instagram or current website
   > 3. two or three websites you love the look of
   >
   > We'll send you a preview of your new site. No cost, no obligation.
2. **Comment to message:** when someone comments `DEMO` on the reel, carousel or pricing post, it sends them the same DM. That's why the captions say "Comment DEMO". Comments push reach, and the DM goes out by itself.
3. **Instant reply:** a greeting for anyone messaging for the first time, which points them to the same three questions.

Bio (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook):
