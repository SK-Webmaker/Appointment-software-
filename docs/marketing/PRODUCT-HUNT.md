**Description (238/260):**

Kairo runs a salon's bookings, deposits, reminders and client records (colour formulas, patch tests, consent, photos) with a free iPhone app. Instead of a monthly subscription it's A$400 once, or 3 × A$150, with no commission on bookings.

**Why these choices**

- **Tagline:** it leads with who it's for and the one thing nobody else
  offers (paying once). People on Product Hunt scan taglines in a second.
- **Gallery:** image 1 is the hook (price plus the real app). Images 2–4
  each answer one objection: how clients book, no-shows, client records.
  Image 5 restates the price with the 14-day refund.
- **Sign-up link in the first comment:** it carries `from=producthunt`, so
  a sale from the launch shows up in the daily sale check as
  `signup: paid … from producthunt`.

## Before launch day (now until 30 Nov)

1. Finish the free listings in `SEO-PLAN.md`, especially SaaSHub and
   AlternativeTo. A launch with a few existing reviews and links converts
   better.
2. Line up 20–40 real people to look on the day: the two salon customers,
   friends, Instagram followers and anyone who has replied to outreach.
   Send them the link when it's live. Ask them to "check it out and leave
   honest feedback". **Never ask for upvotes.** Product Hunt penalises
   that, and vote rings get a launch removed.
3. On 30 Nov, post an Instagram story: "Launching on Product Hunt tomorrow
   night 🚀".

## Launch day (Tue 1 Dec, Melbourne time)

- **7:01 pm:** it goes live. Post the first comment straight away. Share
  the link on Instagram (story plus a post) and send it to the people from
  step 2.
- **7 pm–11 pm:** reply to every comment within about 30 minutes. Answer
  objections plainly, using the pricing and "what it doesn't do" lines.
- **6 am–2 pm Wed:** this is the US afternoon, when most votes and comments

- **The day after:** thank everyone in the comments. Add "Featured on
  Product Hunt" to the site footer if it gets featured. Log the results
  in `LOG.md`.

## Honest expectation

A first launch with no existing audience usually lands a few dozen upvotes,
a backlink from producthunt.com and some curious visitors. The main win
here is the link and the credibility. Most people on Product Hunt don't run
salons. The daily sale check reports any sign-up `from producthunt`; if
one comes, it's a bonus.
