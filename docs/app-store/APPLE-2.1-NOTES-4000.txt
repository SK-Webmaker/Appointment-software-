2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Kairo is an appointment book for small salons and barbershops, typically one to
five staff, who today run on paper diaries or on commission-taking marketplace
apps. Kairo gives them their own booking page on their own address, a diary
built for real salon work, client records with history, and invoicing.

It is bought once on kairobookings.com. No subscription, no commission. This
app is the free phone companion to that account: it sells nothing and is
sign-in only.

Audience: any salon, barber, beauty, nail or massage business that takes
appointments. Sold publicly to anyone, not to a single company or a closed
group of employees, so it belongs on the public App Store.

3. SETTING UP AND ACCESSING FEATURES
  Salon address: demo   (resolves to demo.kairobookings.com)
  Email:         demo@kairobookings.com
  Password:      <DEMO PASSWORD>

On first launch the app asks for the salon's Kairo address. Enter "demo", then
sign in. No sample files needed.

The demo salon is fully populated - two stylists, a service menu, fourteen
clients, a year of history - so every screen has content. All of it is
invented: no real business's data and no real person's information appears.

  Calendar - a week across two stylists. Tap an empty slot to book.
  Clients  - visit history, totals, notes.
  Invoices - build one from an appointment, record a payment.
  Settings > Account > Delete my account - in-app deletion per 5.1.1(v). Safe
             to run on the demo; we restore it.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
  Hosting/database  Render (per-business databases, Australia)
  Push              Apple Push Notification service
  Email             Resend
  SMS               ClickSend (or Twilio/Telnyx, if the business connects one)
  Card payments     Stripe or Square, connected to the BUSINESS's own account.
                    Card details never reach Kairo's servers, and Kairo never
                    holds or routes the money.
  Bot protection    Cloudflare Turnstile, public booking page only
  DNS/email routing Cloudflare
  Password safety   Have I Been Pwned range API (hash prefix only)

No analytics, advertising SDKs, crash-reporting SDKs, data brokers or AI
services of any kind.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app behaves identically in every region. English only, prices display
in the business's configured currency, no feature varies by country.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY / PROTECTED MATERIAL
Not a regulated industry app and not a medical device. It does not diagnose,
treat or monitor, and makes no health claims.

Health is declared in App Privacy for one reason: a salon can type free-text
notes against a client, and those sometimes record an allergy or patch-test
result before a colour service. Australia's Privacy Act treats that as health
information, so we declared it. Kairo stores it for the business, never reads
it and never shares it.
https://kairobookings.com/legal/privacy

No third-party copyrighted material: all content is the business's own data
plus our own interface. Support: support@kairobookings.com
