WHAT CHANGED IN BUILD 1.0.0 (19)
The previous build opened on a screen asking for the salon's Kairo address.
An address that was not a salon showed "No salon at this address", and because
it was saved, every relaunch returned to that page. Fixed: the app now opens
on an email and password sign-in. There is no address to type. If a saved
business can no longer be found, the app returns to sign-in by itself, and
with no connection it shows "Can't reach Kairo" with a Try again button.

1. HOW TO REVIEW
Open the app. The first screen is "Sign in to Kairo".
  Email:     demo@kairobookings.com
  Password:  <DEMO PASSWORD>
Tap Sign in. The app opens the demo business (Luxe Hair Studio) and remembers
it; later launches go straight in. Notifications are asked for after sign-in.

The demo business is fully populated - three stylists, thirteen services,
fourteen clients, past history and six weeks of upcoming bookings - so every
screen has content. All of it is sample data: no real business or client.

  Calendar - tap the menu, then Calendar. Tap an empty slot to book.
  Clients  - visit history, totals, notes.
  Billing  - build an invoice from an appointment, record a payment.
  Account  - "Closing your account" > Close my account: in-app deletion per
             5.1.1(v), confirmed with the password and the business name.
             Please stop at the final button if you can: completing it
             really closes this demo business for later reviews.
  Sign out - in the menu; the app returns to the sign-in screen.

3. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Kairo is an appointment book for small salons and barbershops, typically one
to five staff. It gives them their own booking page, a diary built for salon
work, client records with history, and invoicing.

It is bought once on kairobookings.com. No subscription, no commission. This
app is the free phone companion to that account: it sells nothing, links to
nothing that sells, and is sign-in only.

Audience: any salon, barber, beauty, nail or massage business that takes
appointments. Sold publicly, not to one company or a closed group, so it
belongs on the public App Store.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
  Hosting/database  Render (per-business databases, Singapore)
  Push              Apple Push Notification service
  Email             Resend
  SMS               ClickSend (or Twilio if the business connects one)
  Card payments     Stripe or Square, connected to the BUSINESS's own account.
                    Card details never reach Kairo's servers, and Kairo never
                    holds or routes the money.
  Cloudflare        DNS, email routing, and bot checks on some forms
  Password safety   Have I Been Pwned range API (hash prefix only)

No analytics, advertising SDKs, crash-reporting SDKs, data brokers or AI
services of any kind.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY / PROTECTED MATERIAL
Not a regulated industry app and not a medical device. No health claims.

Health is declared in App Privacy for one reason: a salon can type free-text
notes against a client, which sometimes record an allergy or patch-test
result. Australia's Privacy Act treats that as health information, so we
declared it. Kairo stores it for the business and never reads or shares it.
