# Kairo policies — draft statements

*Drafts, written at the owner's direction as brief, plain statements rather
than lawyer-reviewed contracts. They are not legal advice. Each is short on
purpose: a policy nobody reads protects nobody. Fill in the bracketed items
before publishing.*

---

## Terms of Service

**Who we are.** Kairo is booking, payments and client-management software
sold by [legal name, ABN], Australia.

**What you buy.** A one-off licence, A$410 on our website or A$519.99 through
the App Store, to run one Kairo for one business, for as long as we operate
the service. There is no monthly fee and no commission. Hosting, updates,
backups and the app are included.

**What you provide.** Email, text and card-payment accounts are yours
(Resend, ClickSend, Stripe or Square). You pay those providers directly; we
add nothing and hold none of that money.

**Your data.** Your clients, bookings, invoices and records belong to you.
You can download a complete copy at any time from Settings → Backups and
export your clients as a spreadsheet. We do not sell, share or use your data
for anything except running your Kairo.

**Our access.** Our systems can read your Kairo's file in order to host it,
back it up, restore it and fix faults. We look inside only when you ask us
to or when a fault requires it, and we never copy it elsewhere except for
backups.

**Your responsibilities.** Keep your password private. Send messages only to
people who have dealt with your business or agreed to hear from you. Follow
the Spam Act and ACMA's sender-ID rules for texts. Do not use Kairo for
anything unlawful.

**Suspension.** We may suspend a Kairo that is used for spam, fraud or
unlawful activity, or where a payment is disputed. We will tell you why and
how to resolve it. Your data stays intact for at least 90 days.

**Ending.** You can delete your business at any time from the Account page;
we email you a full copy first. If we ever stop operating the service we
will give at least 90 days' notice and a full export.

**Liability.** To the extent the law allows, our liability is limited to the
amount you paid us. Nothing here removes rights you have under the
Australian Consumer Law.

**Law.** Victoria, Australia.

## Refund Policy

**14 days, no reason needed.** Within 14 days of paying, request a refund
from your Account page and we refund the full amount automatically. We email
you a copy of your data, then delete your Kairo 30 days later.

**After 14 days.** No refunds for change of mind. If Kairo has a major fault
we cannot fix, the Australian Consumer Law gives you a refund and we honour
it — write to [support email].

**Bought through the App Store?** Apple handles those refunds under Apple's
policy. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot process or refuse an App
Store refund ourselves.

## Privacy Policy

**What we hold about you (the business owner):** your name, email, mobile
number, ABN and business name; a record of your payment (never your card
number — Stripe or Apple holds that); your Kairo's address and plan; your
device's push-notification token if you use the app; support messages.

**Why:** to create and run your Kairo, verify who you are, take payment,
send you service messages and push notifications, and answer support.

**What we hold about your clients:** nothing in our own systems. Your
clients' details live only in your Kairo's own file, which we host for you.
See *Data handling for your clients' information* below.

**Sharing:** with the providers needed to run the service — our hosting
provider (Render), our DNS and edge provider (Cloudflare), Stripe for
payments, Apple for App Store purchases and push notifications, and the
Australian Business Register to check your ABN. Never sold.

**Keeping and deleting:** for as long as you have a Kairo, then 90 days.
Delete your business from the Account page and everything above goes with
it, after we email you your copy.

**Security:** encrypted in transit and at rest; passwords hashed; no secrets
sent to browsers; a separate file per business.

**If something goes wrong:** we tell you within 72 hours of learning of a
breach that affects you, and what to do.

**Your rights:** ask us what we hold, correct it, or delete it — [support
email]. Complaints can also go to the OAIC (oaic.gov.au).

## Data handling for your clients' information

Your Kairo may hold your clients' names, contact details, appointment
history, notes and — if you use treatment records — health information such
as allergies, patch tests and consents. Under the Privacy Act, a business
that holds health information is responsible for it regardless of size.

- We host your file on infrastructure in Singapore (or Australia where
  available), encrypted at rest, with daily snapshots and an emailed backup
  to you.
- We access it only to operate, back up, restore or repair the service.
- You decide what to collect, who in your team sees it, and when to delete
  it. Kairo keeps the record; it never decides whether a treatment is safe.
- If you close your business or leave Kairo, you receive the full file and we
  delete our copies after 90 days.
- Tell your clients: a short privacy notice for your booking page is below.

## Acceptable Use

Kairo may not be used to send unsolicited marketing, to message people who
have opted out, to impersonate another business, or for anything unlawful.
Texts must comply with the Spam Act 2003 and ACMA sender-ID registration.
Repeated complaints from recipients lead to suspension of messaging first
and of the Kairo second, with notice and a chance to fix it.
