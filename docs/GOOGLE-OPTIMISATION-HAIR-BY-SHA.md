# Google Optimisation — Hair by Sha, Camberwell

**Business:** Hair By Sha - Camberwell · 1 Prospect Hill Rd, Camberwell VIC 3124
**Owner/stylist:** Shamalka Kiridena · 0452 611 799
**Audit date:** 16 September 2026
**Audited by:** Kairo

## 1. The short version

Someone has pitched Sha on "better exposure for her Google profile, through Google Maps."

**The premise is correct.** For a suburban salon, the Google Maps local pack is where nearly all
discovery-stage demand lands. That is the right thing to work on.

**The framing is usually wrong.** There is no product to buy here. Google's own documentation says
it plainly:

> "There's no way to request or pay for a better local ranking on Google."
> — [Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091)

Everything that actually moves Maps rankings is free, is done inside the profile itself, and takes a
few focused hours. Below is what I found, what it's costing her, and exactly what to do.

### Her actual position today

I ran a live geo-grid — the same query from different points around Camberwell — because that is the
only honest way to measure "Maps exposure."

| Query | Search point | Distance from her door | Her position |
|---|---|---|---|
| `balayage Camberwell` | Camberwell Junction | ~0 km | **#2 of 20** |
| `foils highlights hairdresser` | Canterbury | ~2 km east | **#10 of 20** |
| `hair colour specialist` | Hawthorn | ~2 km west | **Not in top 20** |

**This is the entire problem in one table.** Her profile is not broken — at her own front door she
beats every salon in Camberwell except one, with a perfect 5.0 rating. But her visibility radius is
roughly two kilometres wide, and it falls off a cliff at the edges.

She is not losing to better salons. She is losing to distance.

So the real objective is not "rank higher." It is **widen the radius** — get found in Hawthorn, Glen
Iris, Canterbury and Surrey Hills, which are precisely the suburbs her own website says clients
already travel in from.

## 2. How Google Maps ranking actually works

Google names three factors:

| Factor | What it means | Can Sha change it? |
|---|---|---|
| **Relevance** | How well the profile matches what was typed | **Yes — heavily** |
| **Distance** | How far she is from the searcher | **No** |
| **Prominence** | How well-known the business is | **Yes — over time** |

Distance is fixed. She cannot move the shop. Which means **every metre of extra radius has to be
bought with Relevance and Prominence.** That is the whole strategy.

The independent picture is consistent. Whitespark's 2026 Local Search Ranking Factors survey (47
practitioners scoring 187 factors) weights the categories roughly: **Google Business Profile signals
~32%, review signals ~20%, on-page signals ~15%** — and eight of the top ten Local Pack factors come
straight from the profile itself.

Their top factors, with Sha's current standing against each:

| # | Factor | Sha today |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Primary GBP category | ✅ "Hairdresser" — correct |
| 2 | Proximity of address to point of search | ⚠️ Fixed — cannot change |
| 3 | Keywords in GBP business title | ⚠️ "Hair By Sha - Camberwell" — has the suburb (see §6) |
| 4 | Physical address in city of search | ✅ |
| 5 | **Business is open at time of search** | ❌ **Broken — see 1.1** |
| 6 | High numerical rating (4–5) | ✅ 5.0 — best in class |
| 7 | Address showing (not service-area) | ✅ |
| 8 | **Additional GBP categories** | ❌ **None set** |
| 9 | Quantity of native reviews with text | ⚠️ 35 |
| 10 | **Proper placement of the map pin** | ⚠️ **Shared address, unverified** |
| 11 | **Recency of reviews** | ⚠️ Intermittent |
| 14 | **Sustained influx of reviews (not bursts)** | ⚠️ No system |
| 15 | **HTML NAP matching GBP NAP** | ❌ **Mismatch — see 3.3** |
| 16 | **Completeness of GBP** | ❌ **Large gaps** |
| 17 | Keywords in landing page title tag | ✅ Good |
| 18 | **Geographic keyword relevance of content** | ⚠️ Camberwell only |

### 1.1 The hours problem — the single highest-impact fix

"Business is open at time of search" is the **#5 local ranking factor**, and Google filters closed
businesses out of "open now" and many "near me" searches.

| Day | Google says | Her website says | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monday | Closed | Closed | — |
| Tuesday | 11am–5pm | 11am–4pm | Google says open 1h too late |
| Wednesday | 11am–5pm | 11am–6pm | **1h invisible** |
| Thursday | 11am–5pm | 11am–7pm | **2h invisible** |
| Friday | 11am–5pm | 11am–7pm | **2h invisible** |
| Saturday | 11am–5pm | 9am–5pm | **2h invisible** |
| Sunday | **Closed** | **11am–4pm** | **5h invisible — entire day** |

**She is open roughly 12 hours a week that Google believes she is closed** — including all day Sunday
and both late evenings, which are the most bookable hours a working-client salon has. And Tuesday is
overstated by an hour, which risks someone arriving to a locked door.

> ✅ **Confirmed: the website hours are the correct ones.** Google's are stale. The profile needs to be
> updated to match: Mon closed · Tue 11am–4pm · Wed 11am–6pm · Thu 11am–7pm · Fri 11am–7pm ·
> Sat 9am–5pm · Sun 11am–4pm.

### 3.2 The shared premises — an under-appreciated risk

1 Prospect Hill Rd is a **chair-rental salon**. Hair Hut Camberwell rents chairs (publicly advertised
at $95–$110/day). Three separate businesses share that one address with no differentiation:

| Business | Rating | Address string |
|---|---|---|
| Hair By Sha - Camberwell | 5.0 (35) | 1 Prospect Hill Rd |
| Hair Hut Camberwell Hair & Beauty | 4.6 (49) | 1 Prospect Hill Rd |
| Camberwell Barber Club | 4.3 (129) | 1 Prospect Hill Rd |

Two consequences:

**a) Merge/suspension risk and a vague pin.** Google allows co-located businesses their own profiles,
but expects them to be distinguishable. Compare the peer building at 149 Camberwell Rd, where every
independent operator carries a unit designator — **Breanna Blonde Colourist at "Shop 1/", LIORA LUXE
at "Studio 14/", Studio Deja Wu at "Studio 4/"**. All three out-travel Sha in the geo-grid.

**b) Review leakage.** Reviews explicitly praising Shamalka are sitting on *Hair Hut's* profile and on
a Birdeye page for "Camberwell Hair Salon (By Your Hair Hut)" (4.7, 60 reviews). Clients who walk in
through the host salon's branding review the host salon. Those reviews build a competitor's
prominence, and Google will not transfer them.

### 3.3 NAP consistency — real conflicts found

Name/Address/Phone consistency is ranking factor #15 and an entire factor category. Three conflicts:

1. **Her own website footer** reads `1 Prospect Hill Rd, Melbourne VIC 3124` — **"Camberwell" is
   missing.** Her schema markup and FAQ both say Camberwell correctly. The visible footer is the one
   that's wrong.
2. **The legacy "Infusion Hair" identity is still live.** Sha traded as Infusion Hair (1300 Centre Rd,
   Clayton) before moving to her own studio in April 2023. The Facebook page "Infusion hair Clayton"
   **still carries her current mobile, 0452 611 799, against the Clayton address.** Yelp, Bookwell and
   WordOfMouth all still list Infusion Hair at Clayton. Phone number is one of the strongest entity
   keys Google has — hers currently points at two different suburbs.
3. **Two duplicate Fresha listings** exist for Hair by Sha, with differing address formats.

### 3.4 Website

The website is genuinely good — fast, well-structured, strong title tag, real FAQ content, valid
`HairSalon` schema. Specific gaps:

- Footer NAP missing "Camberwell" (above)
- Schema `sameAs` lists **only Instagram** — missing Facebook, TikTok, and the Google profile
- Schema has no `hasMap` pointing at the Google listing
- Schema `foundingDate: "2026"` is wrong — the studio opened April 2023, and she has 20+ years behind
  the chair
- **No suburb pages** for Hawthorn, Glen Iris, Canterbury, Surrey Hills — the exact places the
  geo-grid shows her disappearing
- Two Instagram accounts split the signal: `@hairbysha_c` (linked on site) and `@infusion_hair_`
  (legacy handle, already renamed to "Hair by Sha", carries the older following)

## 4. The action plan

### Tier 1 — this week. Free. Highest impact.

**1. Correct the opening hours.** *(15 min)*
GBP → Edit profile → Hours. Set Google to match the website exactly — these are the confirmed correct
hours: **Mon closed · Tue 11–4 · Wed 11–6 · Thu 11–7 · Fri 11–7 · Sat 9–5 · Sun 11–4.**
**Why:** ranking factor #5, and it recovers ~12 hours a week of peak discoverability, including all
of Sunday. This is the single biggest return on 15 minutes available anywhere in this document.

**2. Add the booking link.** *(10 min)*
GBP → Edit profile → Bookings → paste `https://hairbysha-booking.onrender.com/book`
**Why:** eight of her top-20 competitors show a "Book online" button and she doesn't. Manual booking
links are free and go live in 24–48h. The native "Reserve with Google" button requires a partner
provider — the manual link is the free path and works now.
**How it works:** it converts profile views into bookings without a second click-through, and
click-through rate from Maps is itself ranking factor #13.

**3. Write the business description.** *(20 min)*
750 characters, currently empty. Draft ready to paste:

> Hair by Sha is a one-chair colour studio in Camberwell, run by Shamalka Kiridena. After twenty years
> behind the chair, she works with one client at a time — the same hands from consultation through to
> blow wave, with no hand-off to an apprentice.
>
> The work is mostly colour: full and half-head foils, hand-painted balayage, root touch-ups, grey
> blending, toners and glosses, alongside precision cuts, blow waves and K18 bond treatments. Colour
> correction and occasion styling are booked by consultation.
>
> Appointments are unhurried and quoted before any colour is mixed. Clients travel from Hawthorn, Glen
> Iris, Canterbury and Surrey Hills. Street parking on Prospect Hill Rd.

**Why:** it reads naturally but carries every service term and every target suburb. Note what it is
*not* — a keyword list. "Keyword stuffing the GBP description" is an explicit **negative** ranking
factor.

**4. Add secondary categories.** *(5 min)*
Keep **Hairdresser** as primary. Add **Hair salon**. Add **Beauty salon** only if she genuinely offers
beauty services.
**Why:** ranking factor #8, completely unused. Google's guidance is "the fewest number of categories it
takes to describe your core business" — so resist adding more than is true.

**5. Add Services.** *(45 min)*
GBP → Edit profile → Services. Every service gets a name, price and ~300-char description:

| Service | From |
|---|---|
| Ladies Cut & Blow Wave | $110 |
| Blow Wave | $55 |
| Root Colour + Refresh | $150 |
| Partial Foils | $240 |
| Full Head Foils | $320 |
| Balayage / Lived-in Blonde | $340 |
| Blonde Transformation + K18 | $420 |
| K18 Bond Treatment | $45 |
| Grey Blending | consultation |
| Colour Correction | consultation |
| Toner / Gloss | consultation |
| Styling & Hair-Up | POA |

**Why this matters more than it looks:** Google shows auto-generated keyword chips on every profile,
drawn from review text and profile content. Hers currently read *"hair styling 9, talent 4, honest
advice 3, blonde colouring 2, blow waves 2."* **No balayage. No foils. No grey blending. No colour
correction.** The salon ranking above her has *"balayage 3"* in its chips. Her single highest-value
service is invisible to Google as a service.

### Tier 2 — next 30 days

**6. Resolve the address ambiguity.** If her space has a real room, studio or suite designation, add it
to the profile so it reads distinctly from the other two businesses at that address. Then check the map
pin sits on the actual entrance clients use. **She must not invent a suite number that doesn't exist** —
that is a suspension trigger, not a shortcut.

**7. Build a review engine — and fix *what* the reviews say.** Her 35 reviews at 5.0 are excellent. The
problem is content, not quality: they praise *her*, not the *services*. Ask at the chair, at the moment
of handover, naming the service: *"If you're happy with it, would you mind mentioning the balayage in a
Google review?"*

Her direct review link (skips search, lands straight on the write-review box):

```
https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=ChIJ89jBWLJB1moRbXDfvygonDI
```

Her direct profile link:

```
https://maps.google.com/?cid=3646833953746153581
```

Put the review link in the Kairo post-appointment message. Target **2–4 per month, sustained** —
ranking factor #14 is explicitly "sustained influx of reviews over time (**rather than bursts**)", and
review recency is a top-five factor for 2026. A steady trickle beats a burst, and a burst looks like
manipulation.

> ⚠️ Never buy, incentivise, or gate reviews (gating = only asking happy clients). All three violate
> Google's policy and risk removal of the reviews and suspension of the profile.

**8. Clean up the legacy Infusion Hair footprint.** Update or unpublish the "Infusion hair Clayton"
Facebook page and remove her mobile number from anything carrying the Clayton address. If Infusion Hair
still trades under someone else, she only needs her number off it. Then chase Yelp, Bookwell and
WordOfMouth.

**9. Merge the duplicate Fresha listings**, and decide whether she is on Fresha *or* Kairo — running
both splits her booking signal and confuses which link Google trusts.

**10. Consolidate Instagram.** Pick one primary account, point the other at it, and list every real
profile in the website's schema.

### Tier 3 — the ongoing engine (90 days)

**11. Photos: 16 → 60+.** Three to five real photos a week from the chair, with client consent. Recency
counts.
> ⚠️ **Do not use AI-generated photos.** "Presence of AI-generated photos/videos on profile" is an
> explicit **negative** ranking factor in the 2026 survey. Several agencies now supply these.
> ℹ️ Geotagging photos does nothing — Google strips EXIF on upload. Anyone selling it is selling air.

**12. A Google Post most weeks.** Short and human — a finished colour, a quiet Tuesday slot, a seasonal
note. "Keyword stuffing Google Posts" is also an explicit negative factor.

**13. Seed the Q&A.** She has zero. Owners are allowed to post *and answer* their own questions. Lift
eight straight from her website FAQ: parking, first appointment, balayage frequency, how pricing is
decided, how long a full head of foils takes.

**14. Suburb pages on the website** — Hawthorn, Glen Iris, Canterbury, Surrey Hills. **This is the main
radius lever.** Proximity cannot be changed, so geographic relevance in content (factor #18) has to do
the work instead. These must be genuinely useful pages — travel time, parking, which of her services
suit that client — not thin doorway pages, which Google penalises.

**15. Citations.** Consistent NAP across Localsearch, Yellow Pages AU, TrueLocal, Hotfrog, Yelp AU,
Bing Places, Facebook, and **Apple Business Connect** (Apple Maps is a genuine gap for most small AU
salons).

**16. Website fixes** — see §5.

## 5. Website changes — done and live

Applied and verified live on `hairbyshacamberwell.com` on 17 September 2026:

- ✅ **Footer NAP corrected** to `1 Prospect Hill Rd, Camberwell VIC 3124`, matching the Google profile
  character for character. Zero occurrences of the old `Prospect Hill Rd, Melbourne` remain anywhere
  on the site.
- ✅ **Address moved to a single source of truth** (`src/data/site.ts`), so every page, the schema and
  the FAQ now read the same string. This is what stops the drift recurring.
- ✅ **`sameAs` extended** with Facebook, TikTok and the Google Business Profile
- ✅ **`hasMap`** added, pointing at the verified profile link
- ✅ **`foundingDate` corrected** from 2026 to 2023
- ✅ **`Person` entity added** for Shamalka Kiridena — `founder` and `employee`, cross-referenced by
  `@id`, which helps Google tie the stylist to the business as one entity
- ✅ **Sub-pages now reference the canonical business by `@id`** instead of duplicating the whole
  business object on every page
- ✅ **Four suburb pages built and live** — `/hawthorn`, `/glen-iris`, `/canterbury`, `/surrey-hills`.
  Each has its own angle, travel description, service emphasis and testimonial set rather than one
  template with the suburb swapped. Each is honest that a 4–6 week root touch-up may suit a closer
  salon, which is what keeps them useful pages rather than doorway pages.
- ✅ **Added to `sitemap.xml`** and linked from every page under an "Areas we look after" footer heading

**Not done, deliberately:** embedding the review link on a post-visit page. That belongs with the
booking flow rather than the marketing site, so it is better handled in Kairo's post-appointment
message (Tier 2, item 7).

## 6. What to say to the agency

The Maps premise is right. Judge them on which of these two lists they're selling.

### ✅ Legitimate — and all of it free

Everything in §4. If they'll do it properly, competently and consistently, that has real value — most
owners never find the time. Pay for **execution and consistency**, not for access or influence.

### 🚩 Red flags — walk away

| Claim | Reality |
|---|---|
| "Guaranteed #1 on Maps" | Google: *"There's no way to request or pay for a better local ranking."* |
| "We'll optimise your business name to *Hair by Sha \| Balayage & Colour Specialist Camberwell*" | Keyword-stuffing the business name is a **top suspension trigger**. The name must match real-world signage. |
| "We'll get you 50 reviews fast" | Bought, incentivised or gated reviews → reviews removed, profile suspended. Bursts look like manipulation. |
| "We'll add professional AI images" | Explicit **negative** ranking factor in 2026. |
| "We'll geotag your photos" | Google strips EXIF. Does nothing. |
| "We'll add extra listings to cover more suburbs" | Fake locations are map spam and a hard-suspension trigger. |
| "We'll set your service area to all of Melbourne" | "Service areas larger than 2 hours' driving distance" is a **negative** factor. |

**One important nuance on the business name.** Ranking factor #3 really is "keywords in the GBP business
title", and it's why *Breanna Blonde Colourist* and *Mark L Hair – Hair Colour Specialist in Melbourne*
travel further than Sha does. That is legitimate for them because it is genuinely their trading name.
If Sha wanted to rebrand to something like *Sha Hair Colour Studio* — on her signage, her website, her
socials, her invoices — she could carry that to Google honestly. Changing it on Google alone, while the
door still says Hair by Sha, is the suspension route. **Real rebrand: fine. Google-only rename: not.**

Also switch on the profile's own performance reporting — calls, bookings, direction requests, and the
split between **discovery** searches (people who didn't know her) and **direct** searches (people who
looked her up by name). Discovery growth is the number that proves the radius is widening.

### What to expect, honestly

- **Hours + booking link + description + services:** effect within days to ~3 weeks. Booking enquiries
  should move first, before rankings do.
- **Reviews, photos, posts:** compounding over 2–3 months.
- **Suburb pages and citations:** 3–6 months. This is the slow lever, and the one that actually buys
  radius.

Nothing here makes her #1 in Hawthorn next week. The realistic goal is to convert a two-kilometre
radius into a four-to-five kilometre one over a couple of quarters, while capturing far more of the
demand already reaching her at the centre.
