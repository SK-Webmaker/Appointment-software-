# Video guide: "Put your new website online" ($350 Website Only clients)

**What this video is for:** the client has paid $350 and received two files. This video takes them from those files
to their website live on their own web address. No technical knowledge is assumed.

**Length:** about 6–8 minutes. **Tone:** calm, friendly, slow, like a helpful friend sitting next to them.
**Format:** a screen recording of a real computer with a voiceover. Zoom in on every button before it's clicked,
and highlight it with a circle or arrow. Keep each step's caption on screen until the step is done.

**Before recording:** have ready a sample `yourbusiness-website.zip`, a fresh Netlify account (or
incognito window), and a domain registrar account (Namecheap is used below). Blur any real emails or card details.

**Never show or say:** Lovable, AI, Claude, or anything about how the site was built.

---

## Chapter 1: What you've got (0:00–0:45)

**On screen:** the delivery email, then the two files in the Downloads folder.

**Voiceover:**
> "Congratulations, your new website is ready, and it's 100% yours. In this video we'll put it online,
> step by step. It takes about 10 minutes, it's free, and you don't need any technical skills.
> In your email you got two files. The first one, ending in **website**, is your website. That's the one
> we'll use today. The second one, ending in **code**, is a backup. Save it somewhere safe, like Google Drive,
> and if you ever hire someone to change your site, give them that file."

**Caption:** `You need: the "website" file. Keep the "code" file safe as a backup.`

---

## Chapter 2: Unzip your website (0:45–1:45)

**On screen:** split screen, Windows on the left, Mac on the right.

**Voiceover:**
> "Your website comes zipped up in one file, like a parcel. Let's open it.
> **On Windows:** right-click the website file, click **Extract All**, then click **Extract**.
> **On a Mac:** just double-click it.
> You'll now see a normal folder with the same name. That folder *is* your website."

**Caption:** `Windows: right-click → Extract All · Mac: double-click`

**Tip on screen:** `See a folder (not a zip)? You're ready.`

---

## Chapter 3: Make your free Netlify account (1:45–2:45)

**On screen:** browser → **netlify.com** → **Sign up** → **Sign up with Google** (or email).

**Voiceover:**
> "Next, we need somewhere for your website to live. We'll use Netlify. It's a trusted company that hosts
> millions of websites, and it's free for a site like yours.
> Go to **netlify.com** and click **Sign up**. The easiest way is **Sign up with Google**, using your
> business email. If it asks a few questions about you, just answer them or click skip.
> You're in. This account is yours, in your name. Nobody else controls it."

**Caption:** `netlify.com → Sign up → Sign up with Google`

---

## Chapter 4: Put your website online (2:45–4:00)

**On screen:** go to **app.netlify.com/drop**. Drag the *unzipped folder* from the desktop onto the page.
Show the upload, then the green "published" result and the link ending in `.netlify.app`.

**Voiceover:**
> "This is the fun part. While you're logged in, go to **app.netlify.com/drop**.
> You'll see a box that says to drag and drop your folder. Grab the **folder** we unzipped earlier,
> the folder and not the zip file, and drop it on that box.
> Wait a few seconds… and that's it. Your website is live on the internet!
> Netlify gives it a random temporary address ending in **netlify.app**. Click it and have a look."

**Caption:** `app.netlify.com/drop → drag your FOLDER in`

**Optional step (show quickly):**
> "Want a nicer temporary name? Go to your site's settings, click **Change site name**, and type something
> like your business name."

*(Netlify sometimes calls a site a "project". Same thing.)*

---

## Chapter 5: Get your own web address (4:00–5:15)

**On screen:** **namecheap.com** → search bar → type a name → **Add to cart** → checkout. Show the auto-renew switch.

**Voiceover:**
> "Right now your site has a temporary address. Let's give it your own, like **yourbusiness.com.au**.
> This is called a domain. It costs about $15 to $25 a year, and you buy it in your own name, so it's yours.
> We'll use Namecheap, but GoDaddy or Cloudflare work the same way.
> Search for the name you want. If it's available, add it to your cart and check out.
> Important: make sure **auto-renew** is turned **on**. If a domain isn't renewed, your website address stops working.
> Already own a domain? Great, skip this and just log in to where you bought it."

**Caption:** `Buy your domain in YOUR name. Turn ON auto-renew.`

**Note for .com.au:** these need an ABN when buying. Have it ready.

---

## Chapter 6: Connect your domain to your website (5:15–7:15)

**On screen (Netlify):** open your site → **Domain management** → **Add a domain** → type the domain → **Verify** → **Add domain**.
Then click **Set up Netlify DNS** (or "Use Netlify DNS") and keep clicking **Continue** until Netlify shows
**4 nameservers** (lines that look like `dns1.p01.nsone.net`).

**Voiceover:**
> "Now we point your new address at your website. Back in Netlify, open your site and click **Domain management**,
> then **Add a domain**. Type your domain exactly as you bought it, and click **Verify**, then **Add domain**.
> Netlify will offer to manage your domain for you. Choose **Netlify DNS** and click **Continue** until you see
> a list of four **nameservers**. Keep this page open. We'll copy these in a second."

**On screen (Namecheap):** **Domain List** → **Manage** next to the domain → **Nameservers** dropdown →
**Custom DNS** → paste all 4 → click the green tick ✓.

**Voiceover:**
> "Now go to Namecheap, open **Domain List**, and click **Manage** next to your domain.
> Find **Nameservers**, change it to **Custom DNS**, and paste in the four nameservers from Netlify, one per line.
> Copy and paste them, don't type them, so there are no typos. Then click the green tick to save.
> On GoDaddy or Cloudflare, look for the same word: **Nameservers**. Choose to use your own or custom ones,
> and paste the four in."

**Caption:** `Copy the 4 nameservers from Netlify → paste into "Custom DNS" at your domain company`

---

## Chapter 7: Wait, then you're done (7:15–8:00)

**On screen:** Netlify Domain management showing the domain with "Pending" → later "HTTPS ✓". Then the live site
on the real domain, with the padlock in the address bar.

**Voiceover:**
> "That's everything. Now the internet needs a little time to catch up. Usually it takes a few minutes to a few hours,
> and occasionally up to a day. Don't worry if your address doesn't work straight away. That's normal.
> Netlify will also add the little **padlock** for security all by itself, once your domain is connected.
> When you type your address in and your website appears, you're live!
> Everything is in your name: your website, your hosting, and your domain. It's all yours.
> Congratulations, and thank you for your business!"

**Caption:** `Can take up to 24 hours. The padlock 🔒 appears automatically.`

## Troubleshooting cards (optional short clips, 15–20 seconds each)

| Problem | Say on screen |
|---|---|
| Site shows a blank page or "Page not found" | "You dragged in the zip file instead of the folder. Unzip it first, then drag the folder." |
| Site looks old after the domain step | "Give it a few hours. Try refreshing, or open it on your phone." |
| Netlify says the domain is "already in use" | "Double-check the spelling. If it's correct, the domain is connected elsewhere. Check with whoever set it up." |
| No padlock after a day | "In Netlify → Domain management → HTTPS, click **Verify DNS configuration**, then **Provision certificate**." |
| Can't find "Nameservers" | "Search your domain company's help page for 'change nameservers'. Every company has one." |
