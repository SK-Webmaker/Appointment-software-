## What they share (the owner's taste)

1. **Type is the hero.** Display sizes 9–20× the body size, tight leading
   (0.8–0.95), negative tracking at scale. Few images; when present, they are
   treated to belong to the palette.
2. **One accent, rationed.** Tinted canvas + tinted ink + a single saturated
   colour (see the `colour-palettes` skill).
3. **Two typographic registers.** Monumental statements and tiny technical
   annotations (mono, uppercase, wide tracking); little in between.
4. **Flat structure.** Hairline rules, colour steps and scale create
   hierarchy, not cards and grey shadows. Glow belongs to the accent only.
5. **Editorial devices.** Rotated margin labels, split headings, numbered
   chapters, stamped mono headings, a signature footer (coordinates, live
   time).

These are references, not rules: take the devices that fit the brand, and
keep the playbook's motion and quality gates (`sites/PLAYBOOK.md`).
