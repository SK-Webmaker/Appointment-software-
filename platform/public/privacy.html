Privacy Policy

What we hold, why, and how to get rid of it.

What we hold about you (the business owner)

Your name, email, mobile number, ABN and business name; a record of your payment (never your card number — Stripe holds that); your Kairo's address and plan; your device's push-notification token if you use the app; and support messages.

Why

To create and run your Kairo, verify who you are, take payment, send you service messages and push notifications, and answer support.

What we hold about your clients

Nothing in our own systems. Your clients' details live only in your Kairo's own file, which we host for you.

Sharing

With the providers needed to run the service — our hosting provider (Render), our DNS and edge provider (Cloudflare), Stripe for payments, Apple for App Store purchases and push notifications, and the Australian Business Register to check your ABN. Never sold.

Keeping and deleting

For as long as you have a Kairo, then 90 days. Delete your business from the Account page and everything above goes with it, after we email you your copy.

Security

Encrypted in transit and at rest; passwords hashed; no secrets sent to browsers; a separate database file per business.

If something goes wrong

We tell you within 72 hours of learning of a breach that affects you, and what to do.

Your clients' information

Your Kairo may hold your clients' names, contact details, appointment history, notes and — if you use treatment records — health information such as allergies, patch tests and consents. Under the Privacy Act, a business that holds health information is responsible for it regardless of size. We host that file, encrypted at rest, with daily snapshots and a backup emailed to you. We access it only to operate, back up, restore or repair the service. You decide what to collect, who sees it, and when to delete it.

Your rights

Ask us what we hold, correct it, or delete it — [support email]. Complaints can also go to the OAIC (oaic.gov.au).
