Things people ask most

I've forgotten my password

Use Forgot password on the sign-in screen. The reset link goes to the email address your Kairo was set up with.

How do I get a copy of my data?

Settings → Backups → Download. That file is your whole salon — clients, appointments, invoices and payments — and it is yours to keep. Clients can also be exported as a spreadsheet from the Clients page.

My confirmations and reminders aren't sending

Check Settings → Messaging. Email sending needs to be connected before Kairo can send on your behalf; the setup checklist on your dashboard walks through it. Texts need your own ClickSend account and an ACMA-compliant sender ID.

Can I change my booking address?

Yes — email us. Your old address keeps working and forwards, so links you have already given customers do not break.

I want a refund

Within 14 days of paying, request it from your Account page and it is refunded in full, no reason needed. See the Refund Policy. If you bought through the App Store, Apple handles it at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Is my clients' information safe?

Every business has its own separate database file — no shared table, no other salon's data alongside yours. It is encrypted at rest, backed up daily, and a copy is emailed to you. The Privacy Policy sets out exactly what is held and by whom.

Still stuck?

Send a screenshot. It is almost always faster than describing the screen, and it means we can usually answer with a fix rather than a question.
