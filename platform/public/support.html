Something is wrong right now

My booking page will not load

First, check the address. It is yourname.kairobookings.com. If you have an older bookmark or a shortcut saved to your phone's home screen, it may point somewhere that no longer exists — open the address fresh and sign in again rather than using the shortcut.

If the correct address shows “Temporarily unavailable”, that is us, not you, and we already know. Email us anyway with your business name so we can tell you directly when it is back.

I cannot sign in

Use Forgot password on the sign-in screen. The reset link goes to the email address your Kairo was set up with. If that inbox is gone, email us from any address and say which business you are — we will verify it another way rather than lock you out of your own bookings.

A booking I just made is not showing

Pull down to refresh, or reload the page. If it is still missing, do not re-enter it a second time — email us first. Two copies of the same appointment is a harder mess to undo than a missing one, and we can see which it is.

Messages to your clients

Confirmations and reminders are not arriving

Open Settings → Messaging. Email has to be connected before Kairo can send anything on your behalf, and the setup checklist on your dashboard walks through it. Texts need your own ClickSend account and a sender ID registered with ACMA.

The Messages page lists every message Kairo has tried to send, whether it went, and the reason if it did not. That page answers this question faster than we can.

A client says they got two of the same message

Tell us — include the client's first name and roughly when. Kairo is built not to do this, so if it happened we want to know why.

Your data

How do I get a copy?

Settings → Backups → Download. That file is your whole business — clients, appointments, invoices and payments — and it is yours to keep, whether or not you carry on using Kairo. Clients can also be exported as a spreadsheet from the Clients page.

Does Kairo back it up for me?

Yes. Kairo emails a copy of your whole business to you on a schedule you choose in Settings → Backups: every day, every week, every fortnight, every month, every second month, or never. New businesses start on weekly.

That emailed copy is the one that matters. Keep a few of them somewhere you control — they are what lets your business be restored no matter what happens to us.

Is my clients' information safe?

Every business has its own separate database file. There is no shared table and no other business's data sitting alongside yours. It is held on an encrypted disk, and the Privacy Policy sets out exactly what is stored, for how long, and who can reach it.

Account and billing

I want a refund

Within 14 days of paying, request it from your Account page and it is refunded in full, no reason needed — see the Refund Policy. After 14 days, email us and say what went wrong; the Australian Consumer Law gives you rights that last a good deal longer than our own promise, and we would rather sort it out than argue about a date.

If you bought through the App Store, Apple handles refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Can I change my booking address?

Email us and we will change it for you. Be aware the old address stops working once it is changed, so do it before you print cards or put it on a shopfront — not after.

I want to stop using Kairo

Download your backup first (Settings → Backups), then email us. There is no subscription to cancel. We keep your data for 30 days after you ask us to close the account, in case you change your mind, and then it is deleted.

Before you email

Your business name, what you tapped, what happened, and a screenshot. With those four things most problems are answered with a fix rather than a question.
