Turn on text reminders
Four steps · about five minutes
Texts go through ClickSend, an Australian SMS company.
You pay them directly, a few cents a text. Kairo adds nothing.
Kairo · Text messages
Before you start

Open Settings, then Messages

Tap Set up text messages. It's on your dashboard's setup list too, as Set up texts.

Step 1 of 4

Open a free ClickSend account

Tap Open ClickSend. It opens in a new tab, and Kairo stays open in this one.

Step 1 of 4

Fill in your details

Your name, business, email and a password — or Sign up with Google. Then tap Start free trial and follow ClickSend's prompts to confirm.

Step 2 of 4

Find your API Key

In ClickSend, tap the key icon at the top right. It shows your Username and API Key. Copy each one.

Step 2 of 4

Paste them into Kairo

Username in the first box, API Key in the second. Tap Connect and Kairo checks them with ClickSend straight away.

Step 3 of 4

Check your credit

Kairo reads your ClickSend balance for you. Free credit covers the test. Before going live, add about $20 — a few hundred texts.

Step 4 of 4

Choose what to text, then test

Reminders and confirmations are ticked for you. Check the mobile number, then tap Turn on texts and send me a test.

Step 4 of 4

Texts are on

They only switch on once ClickSend accepts the test, so nothing goes half-working to your clients.
