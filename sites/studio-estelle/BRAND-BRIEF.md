# Studio Estelle — brand brief

Everything on the site comes from Studio Estelle's public Instagram,
[@studio.estelle_](https://www.instagram.com/studio.estelle_/) (read 7 October
2026): her bio, her introduction post, her "garment hire myths" and "bonds"
carousels, the Nara post, her fitting-room reel and the price list she posts
in her comments. Nothing has been invented.

> Other accounts share the name (a hair studio, a personal account). None of
> their details are used; only posts whose caption is signed by
> @studio.estelle_ count.

## Visual identity

From her posts: candy-pink myth tiles, mint bond tiles, a blush logo with
chocolate-brown type.

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| ivory / stone / card | `#FBF4F2` / `#F6E6E6` / `#FFFDFC` | grounds |
| candy | `#F8B0CB` | the myths chapter (her myth posts) |
| mint | `#E4FBE8` | the bond chapter (her bond posts) |
| ink / ash | `#372412` / `#6B554C` | text |
| rose / plum | `#97455F` / `#7A2E48` | accents (plum on candy) |
| noir / coal | `#2E1D14` / `#3D281B` | dark chapters, buttons |
| pearl / silk | `#F9CFDF` / `#F7B2CD` | accents and buttons on dark grounds |

- **Type:** Bodoni Moda (the serif capitals of her logo), Parisienne (the
  "Studio" script), Jost for text.
- **Shape:** the capsule (a long mirror) and the arch (her fitting-room
  mirror) — every chapter arrives as a sheet with an arched top.
- **Motion:** the Nara stands in a capsule; on scroll it glides to the
  centre and her rail is dealt out around it, then "Any event deserves the
  right outfit" settles; each chapter slides up over the last; the myth cards
  stack; quotes light word by word; the booking arch rises.
