# Kuvaello Posters — brand brief

Everything on the site comes from their own channels (read 12 November
2026): Instagram [@kuvaello_posters](https://www.instagram.com/kuvaello_posters/)
(1,991 followers, 965 posts) and their website
[kuvaelloposters.fi](https://kuvaelloposters.fi/en) (Finnish, English and
Serbian versions). Nothing has been invented.

## Visual identity

Black-and-white mark (a round face with crossed-out eyes beside
"Kuvaello"), dark cinematic posters lit like stadiums, tall condensed
capitals for names. Tokens: paper `#F4F2ED`, black `#0B0B0C`, amber
`#F2A541` (floodlight), burnt amber `#A14A0B` for accents on paper. Type:
Anton (headlines), Montserrat (text).

## Demo choices

- The site shows their prices and links "Order & pay online" to their own
  checkout; the order sheet writes the same details as their form into an
  email or DM, for anyone who'd rather ask first.
- The opening is scroll-driven and plays for everyone, "Reduce motion"
  included (the curtain fades instead of wiping).
