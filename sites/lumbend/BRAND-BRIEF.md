# LUMBEND One — brand brief

**A concept product.** LUMBEND was made up to show how far a one-page site
can go: the lamp, its prices, sizes and specs are fictional, and the site
says so in its footer and in the order sheet. The name was checked against
web search (no company or product uses it). The physics is real: the colour
each noble gas glows in a tube (neon red-orange, argon violet and blue with
mercury, helium pink-peach, krypton silver-white, xenon blue-violet).

## What it is
**LUMBEND One — the real-glass neon desk lamp.** A glass tube bent by hand,
filled with noble gas, on a weighted base. Five shapes (Loop, Bolt, Wave,
Moon, Word) × five gases. Voice: "it", short, confident, a little nocturnal.
Tagline: **Real glass. Real gas. Real glow.**

## Palette (see `.claude/skills/colour-palettes`)
One brand hue — a night violet (H≈290) — tints every neutral. The accent is
**the lamp's own light**: sodium amber until the visitor picks a gas, then
that gas's colour, across the whole page (`--glow`, a registered custom
property, so it fades). Every gas colour was checked as an accent: ≥ 5.5:1 on
void, night and dusk.

| Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Void | `#0b0912` | Canvas (~60%) |
| Night | `#14101e` | Surface step 1 (sheets, cards) |
| Dusk | `#1e1830` | Surface step 2 |
| Bone | `#f2ede4` | Text on dark; light chapter canvas |
| Paper | `#e9e3d8` | Card on bone |
| Haze | `#a59fb8` | Muted text on dark (7.8:1 on void) |
| Smoke | `#5f586e` | Muted text on bone (5.8:1) |
| Glow (amber default) | `#ffb547` | The accent — CTAs, lit words, the band |
| Ember | `#9a4f06` | Accent as text on bone (5.2:1) |
| UV | `#b394ff` | Rare second light |

Gases: Ne `#ff5a36` (6.4:1), Ar `#4fa3ff` (7.6:1), He `#ff9e8a` (9.9:1),
Kr `#e9e2ff` (15.8:1), Xe `#9b8cff` (7.2:1) — ratios on void.

## Type
Syne 400–800 (display, wide caps, tight tracking), Instrument Serif italic
(the "neon script" and human moments), JetBrains Mono (stamped labels and
body). All self-hosted, SIL Open Font License.

## Imagery
None — on purpose. The lamp is drawn in SVG (four stacked strokes make the
glow); everything else is type, light and line.
