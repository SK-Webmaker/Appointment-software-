# NOCTA Light Studio — brand brief

**A concept brand.** NOCTA was made up to show how far a one-page site can
go: the studio, its pieces, places and numbers are fictional, and the site
says so in its footer and in the commission sheet. The physics is real: the
colour each noble gas glows in a tube (neon red-orange, argon violet and blue
with mercury, helium pink-peach, krypton silver-white, xenon blue-violet).

## Who they are
A neon and light studio: hand-bent neon signs, light installations, club and
stage rigs, brand nights. Voice: "we", confident, a little nocturnal, short
sentences. Tagline: **We bend light for a living.**

## Palette (see `.claude/skills/colour-palettes`)
One brand hue — a night violet (H≈290) — tints every neutral; one accent,
Sodium amber, its complement, is rationed to actions, the words that switch
on, and one full-bleed band.

| Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Void | `#0b0912` | Canvas (~60%) |
| Night | `#14101e` | Surface step 1 (sheets, cards) |
| Dusk | `#1e1830` | Surface step 2 |
| Bone | `#f2ede4` | Text on dark; light chapter canvas |
| Paper | `#e9e3d8` | Card on bone |
| Haze | `#a59fb8` | Muted text on dark (7.8:1 on void) |
| Smoke | `#5f586e` | Muted text on bone (5.8:1) |
| Amber | `#ffb547` | The accent (11.3:1 on void) — CTAs, lit words, the band |
| Ember | `#9a4f06` | Accent as text on bone (5.2:1) |
| UV | `#b394ff` | Rare second light (counters, haze) |

Gas colours (`#ff4f2a`, `#4fa3ff`, `#ff9e8a`, `#e9e2ff`, `#9b8cff`) appear
only as the light of the pieces themselves, never as UI.

## Type
Syne 400–800 (display, wide caps, tight tracking), Instrument Serif italic
(the "neon script" and human moments), JetBrains Mono (stamped labels and
body). All self-hosted, SIL Open Font License.

## Imagery
None — on purpose. Every visual is built from type, light and line: neon
signs in CSS, line drawings in SVG, glows as radial gradients.

## To confirm
Nothing — it's fictional. If this ever becomes a real studio, replace the
works, places, counters and the brief's destination.
