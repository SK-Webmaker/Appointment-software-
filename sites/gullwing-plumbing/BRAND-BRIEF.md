# Brand brief — Gullwing Plumbing (concept)

**A concept business** — it exists to show what a trade website can do.
Name checked 22 Nov 2026: "Gullwing Plumbing" appeared only in a name
generator and a demo page.

- **Who:** a 24/7 plumbing outfit for Sydney's Inner West & Eastern Suburbs, est. 2012 (fictional).
- **Promise:** leaks don't wait, neither do we — a fixed price before we start.
- **Voice:** calm under pressure, plain words, no jargon.
- **Palette — blueprint & signal:** concrete canvas, deep-navy night chapters,
  signal orange for actions, water cyan, hi-vis yellow band. Tokens and
  contrast in `src/styles.css`.
- **Type:** Archivo (expanded black capitals for headlines, normal for
  reading); JetBrains Mono for job numbers, gauges and times.
- **Signature motion:** the flood; the filling pipe; flipping cards; the gauge needle.
- **Signature function:** emergency mode, postcode/ETA checker, price gauge,
  job request with job number and .ics.
- **Honesty:** footer, emergency mode, map and confirmation say it's a
  concept; no job is booked. Phone (02) 5550 0199 is in the ACMA fiction range.
