# Brand brief — Saltbush Landscapes (concept)

**A concept business**, made to show what a landscaping website can do.

- **Who:** a native garden designer and planting crew in Sydney's Hills District (fictional).
- **Promise:** gardens that belong here — planted for local clay, hot summers and birds, so they mostly look after themselves.
- **Voice:** warm, practical, quietly opinionated about plants; honest when something won't suit.
- **Palette — the bush in late afternoon:** sandstone paper, eucalypt green
  for actions, burnt ochre for emphasis, saltbush silver and ochre on the
  night chapters. Tokens in `src/styles.css`.
- **Type:** Young Serif (a chunky, seed-packet serif) and Karla.
- **Signature motion:** the garden that grows from dawn to dusk; the turning flower calendar; prints straightening on the pinboard.
- **Signature function:** the plant finder and plant list; the year-in-flower dial; the estimator with a drawn plan; visit booking with .ics.
- **Honesty:** footer and confirmation say it's a concept; phone (02) 5550 0147 is in the ACMA fiction range; email uses `.example`.
