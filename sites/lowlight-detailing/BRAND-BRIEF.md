# Brand brief — Lowlight Detailing (concept)

**A concept business**, made to show what a detailing website can do. Name
checked 23 Nov 2026: no business trading as "Lowlight Detailing" turned up.

- **Who:** a two-bay detailing studio in Alexandria, Sydney, plus a mobile van (fictional).
- **Promise:** detailing, after dark — a dark studio where every swirl shows, so every swirl goes.
- **Voice:** precise, quietly confident, a little technical.
- **Palette — after dark:** studio black and graphite, brushed aluminium for
  light chapters, UV violet for actions, cyan and lilac for light, an
  iridescent "ceramic" gradient used sparingly. Tokens in `src/styles.css`.
- **Type:** Michroma (wide machined capitals) and Geist.
- **Signature motion:** the polish pass; drawn shimmer edges; rolling water beads.
- **Signature function:** the live price/time/ready-by builder; the water-bead demo; booking with ready-by and .ics.
- **Honesty:** footer and confirmation say it's a concept; phone (02) 5550 0163 is in the ACMA fiction range.
