# Brand brief — Squeegee & Sons (concept)

**A concept business**, made to show what a cleaning website can do.

- **Who:** a family house-cleaning business in Sydney's Eastern Suburbs, since 1987 (fictional).
- **Promise:** so clean, you'll walk into it — an exact price up front, the same cleaners each time.
- **Voice:** bright, cheeky, plain about money; family jokes ("Mum answers the phone").
- **Palette — soap and sunshine:** just-washed white, cobalt for actions,
  raspberry for emphasis, sunshine yellow for the band and the booking
  chapter, bubble blue for highlights, deep navy for night. Tokens in
  `src/styles.css`.
- **Type:** Bricolage Grotesque throughout — extra-bold and narrowed for headlines.
- **Look:** photo-led and friendly, like a well-run local cleaning business — real rooms, real kit, plain headings, sentence-case buttons.
- **Signature function:** the instant price; the room-by-room checklist tied to it; the window before-and-after; booking with recurring visits and .ics.
- **Motion (v3):** made to be filmed — a squeegee wipes the steamed-up hero window clean and you can wipe it too; bubbles float up and pop; sparkles trail the mouse; prices roll; the kit slides sideways; booking steps stack. Never at the cost of the booking flow.
- **Honesty:** footer and confirmation say it's a concept; phone (02) 5550 0196 is in the ACMA fiction range; email uses `.example`; no invented reviews or staff.
