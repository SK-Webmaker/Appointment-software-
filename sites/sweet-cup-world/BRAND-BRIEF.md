# Sweet Cup World — brand brief

Everything on the site comes from their public Instagram,
[@sweetcupworld](https://www.instagram.com/sweetcupworld/) (read 7 October
2026; 596 followers, 9 posts). Nothing has been invented. Other accounts
named "Sweet Cup" (e.g. @sweetcup_oc in the US) are unrelated and not used.

## Visual identity

Blush pink cards and menu, a raspberry "Sweet Cup" script over spaced
"WORLD" capitals, gold accents, chocolate. Tokens in `src/styles.css`:
ivory `#FFF7F8`, stone `#FBE7EE`, candy `#F6C3D3`, honey cream `#FFF1D6`,
raspberry `#A1194F`, plum `#861341`, chocolate `#3A2218`, pearl `#FAD3E0`.
Type: Parisienne (script), Bodoni Moda (capitals and headings), Jost (text).
