# Empress Hair — brand brief

Everything on the site comes from Empress Hair's public Instagram,
[@empresshairaus](https://www.instagram.com/empresshairaus/) (read 7 October
2026). The account is new — 85 followers and two posts — so the brief is
short on purpose. Nothing has been invented: where something isn't public
(the stylist's name, a suburb, a menu, prices, hours) the site leaves it out
and the booking message asks the client instead.

> Several other businesses use the name ("Empress Hair & Beauty" in
> Riverside, a Florida braiding salon, hair-extension brands). None of their
> details are used. A search snippet also attached "Located in Lilydale ·
> Call us on 9498-1616" to one of the posts' pages; that text is not in the
> post itself, so it is treated as another account's and left out.

## Their words (quoted on the site)

From the carousel "My Goals as your stylist 🤍":

- **Scalp Health** — "No more gels that leave you with an irritated scalp and
  dull build up. We prioritise moisture through oils and the highest quality
  products to protect your scalp while having your style last."
- **Clean & low-tox styling** — "Instead of putting random chemicals on your
  scalp in the name of effectiveness and/or price, we research each and every
  product and ingredient that touches your head. Our aim is to keep the
  results without compromising your health and wellbeing while promoting
  clean product options into braiding culture."
- **Length Retention** — "We specialise in length retention and protective
  styling that actually helps your hair grow AND keep that length."
- **Comfort & Confidence** — "Everyone who sits in our chair is not only
  provided a service but also is given a comfortable, clean and warming
  environment that keeps you coming back. Our aim is to have women feeling
  their most confident after being in our chair and being able to comfortably
  enjoy their fresh style."

And "Quality > Quantity 🤍 … #melbourne #vic #products #hairstylist" over a
photo of their styling station.

## Visual identity

Monochrome, like the profile mark and the goal slides (grey and stone
grounds, black hair, white script), with one warm taupe for accents.

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| noir / coal | `#0E0D0C` / `#1F1C19` | the profile mark's black — dark chapters |
| ivory / stone / linen | `#F5F1EA` / `#E8E1D6` / `#EEE8E0` | grounds |
| ink / ash | `#161412` / `#5F5850` | text |
| onyx | `#1E1B18` | buttons |
| taupe | `#6B5A47` | accents, italics on light grounds |
| pearl / silk | `#E6DDCF` / `#D9CCB8` | accents and buttons on dark grounds |

- **Type:** Cormorant Garamond (the serif capitals of the mark), Alex Brush
  (the handwriting on the goal slides — used for the goal titles), Jost for
  text.
- **Shape:** the capsule and the arch — a single lock of hair, a braid.
- **Motion:** the curl sits in a capsule; on scroll it glides to the centre
  and the mood board is dealt out around it, then "Quality > Quantity"
  settles; the four goals stack as cards; the product statement lights word
  by word; the booking arch rises out of the dark.

## To confirm with Empress Hair before launch

1. The stylist's name (to add a short intro, and to sign the booking message).
2. The suburb or studio address.
3. The style menu and prices — or confirm they prefer to quote by DM.
4. The days and hours they take bookings (the site only asks the client's preference).
5. That the carousel photos are theirs to use (they may be mood images), and
   any of their own work to add.
6. The domain, once they have one (`url` in `src/site.config.ts`).
