# The Beauty L'atelier — brand brief

Everything on the site comes from the business's own public Instagram,
[@thebeautyatelierrr__](https://www.instagram.com/thebeautyatelierrr__/), read
on 6 October 2026. Nothing has been invented. Where something isn't public
(opening hours, street address, what's inside a "package"), the site leaves
it out rather than guessing.

> **Not to be confused with** `@thebeautyatelierrr` (no underscores), a lash
> and brow studio in Docklands, Melbourne, or `@rose_crown123`

## Visual identity (taken from her graphics and photos)

- **Arch** — her price list sits in an arched ivory card. The arch is the
  site's main shape: frames, cards, the final call to action.
- **Champagne satin** behind the arch.
- **Circular wordmark** — "BEAUTY L'ATELIER" set on a circle beside the title.
- **Type** — a high-contrast serif for titles, light serif capitals for
  section heads, wide-tracked geometric capitals for items. Site uses
  Bodoni Moda (titles), Montserrat (wide-tracked capitals and body) and
  Pinyon Script (Helena's sign-off) to match.
- **Colour**, sampled from her artwork:

| Token | Hex | Source |
|---|---|---|
| ivory | `#FBF9F1` | price-list card `#FEFEF4` |
| bronze | `#957546` | price-list lettering (sampled) |
| bronze-deep | `#7A5E36` | bronze darkened for AA body text on ivory |
| champagne | `#D8CDB8` | satin highlights |
| espresso | `#241A14` | velvet curtain and coffee in the launch carousel |
| gold-soft | `#C9A876` | bronze lifted for use on espresso |

Mood: quiet luxury, black tailoring, gold-rimmed coffee cups, paisley silk,
patent heels, the Harbour at sunset.
