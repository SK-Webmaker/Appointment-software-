# Brand brief — Tidewell Plumbing (concept)

**A concept business**, made to show what a trade website can do.

- **Who:** a family plumbing business on Sydney's Northern Beaches, Manly to Palm Beach (fictional).
- **Promise:** little leak or big one, we're on our way — fixed prices, tidy work, honest advice.
- **Voice:** warm, local, plain-spoken; happy to tell you when it's a job for a kettle and a plunger.
- **Palette — tide and coral:** sea-glass whites, deep teal water for actions
  and dark chapters, coral for emphasis, pale aqua for light on dark.
  Tokens in `src/styles.css`.
- **Type:** Fraunces (soft, wonky optical serif) and DM Sans.
- **Look:** photo-led and plain-spoken, like a well-run local trade business — real photos of the work, sentence-case buttons, no gimmicks.
- **Signature function:** services with "from" prices; common problems by room; honest quick checks; the drip cost calculator; postcode check and job booking with .ics.
- **Honesty:** footer and confirmation say it's a concept; phone (02) 5550 0128 is in the ACMA fiction range; email uses `.example`.
