# Brand brief — Hollis & Grain Barbers (concept)

**A concept business.** Nothing here describes a real shop; it exists to
show what a barbershop website can do. Name checked on 22 Nov 2026: no
business trading as "Hollis & Grain" turned up in search.

- **Who:** a three-chair barbershop in Newtown, Sydney, est. 2019 (fictional).
- **Promise:** sharp cuts, hot towels, no rush — every booking gets its own time.
- **Voice:** a good barber talking: short, warm, a little dry. Signwriter
  capitals for the facts, a serif italic aside for the human bit.
- **Palette — tobacco & brass:** bone canvas, tobacco-black dark chapters,
  pole red for actions, brass for light on the dark. Tokens and contrast
  ratios in `src/styles.css`.
- **Type:** Big Shoulders Display (shopfront signwriting), Instrument Serif
  italic (asides), Hanken Grotesk (reading).
- **Signature motion:** the shop doors parting on scroll; turning pole stripes.
- **Signature function:** live availability (open now, next free chair,
  "Take 2:30 pm"), a four-step booking with validation, reference and a
  calendar file.
- **Honesty:** the footer and the confirmation screen both say it's a
  concept and that no booking is made. Phone (02) 5550 0142 is in the ACMA
  range reserved for fiction; the email uses `.example`.
