# Brand brief — Petalcoat Nail Studio (concept)

**A concept business** — it exists to show what a nail studio website can
do. Name checked 22 Nov 2026: "Petalcoat" only turned up as a clothing item.

- **Who:** a three-artist nail studio in Surry Hills, Sydney, est. 2021 (fictional).
- **Promise:** ten tiny canvases, painted properly — and try the shade first.
- **Voice:** a calm, exacting artist: warm, precise, never gushing.
- **Palette — porcelain & cherry lacquer:** porcelain canvas, blush stone,
  aubergine ink, cherry lacquer for actions, cherry-black chapters, petal
  pink for light on them. Tokens and contrast in `src/styles.css`.
- **Type:** Bodoni Moda (glossy, high contrast) with its italic; Figtree.
- **Signature motion:** the giant nail wearing six shades; the sideways
  sets wall; the sixty-minute clock.
- **Signature function:** the shade bar (live drawn hand), looks that travel
  into the booking, live availability per artist, reference and .ics.
- **Honesty:** footer and confirmation say it's a concept; no booking is
  made. Phone (02) 5550 0187 is in the ACMA fiction range.
