# Hair by Oshi — brand brief

Everything on the site comes from Oshi's own public Instagram,
[@hair_by_oshi_](https://www.instagram.com/hair_by_oshi_/) (read 6 October
2026), plus the Freedom Suites Oakleigh listing for the address. Nothing has
been invented. Where something isn't public (opening times, suite number,
prices), the site leaves it out rather than guessing.

> The earlier Lovable draft of this site ("Oshi's Dark Radiance") contained
> invented details — an FAQ, a "free consultation", "parking at The Links",
> "known across Melbourne". None of it is carried over. An older photo plan
> on the `busy-feynman` branch also pointed at other stylists' posts; every
> photo used now was checked against the post's author (`hair_by_oshi_`).

## Nanoplasty (her explainer carousel and care guide)

- "Nanoplasty uses nano-technology with collagen, amino acids, and proteins to
  penetrate deep into the hair fiber for maximum smoothing … and long-lasting
  restructuring."
- Layers: cuticle (outer), cortex (middle), medulla (inner) — nano-particles
  reach all three; regular treatment molecules are larger, stay on the surface
  and fade faster.
- Process: 1 Wash (opens the cuticle, removes buildup) · 2 Blow dry (heat
  activates the nano-particles) · 3 Flat iron (180–230°C seals the cuticle).
- Care guide: lasts 4–6 months · Day 0 → 1 month smooth & frizz-free → 2–3
  months lasting with care → 4–6 months fades naturally → touch-up · use
  sulfate-free shampoo · wash after 24 hours · heat-styling safe · no special
  maintenance · grows out without damage · safe to colour & bleach.
- Benefits strip: less damage (gentle formula) · long lasting · more shine ·
  safe & effective for most hair types.

> Her two graphics differ slightly on longevity ("up to 3–6 months" vs "lasts
> 4–6 months"). The site uses the care guide's **4–6 months** — confirm.

## Visual identity

The palette is tonal, taken from her work itself rather than her purple logo
(the owner found the purple too loud next to the photos): espresso roots,
chocolate lengths, caramel and honey ribbons, on warm cream. The only nod to
the logo is the heart, in a soft, muted mauve.

| Token | Hex | Source / use |
|---|---|---|
| cocoa | `#4A3328` | chocolate brunette — buttons, the "Oshi" wordmark (10:1 on cream) |
| caramel | `#80593A` | caramel ribbons — accents, eyebrows, italics on light grounds (5.5:1) |
| champagne | `#E3CBA8` | soft blonde — accents on dark grounds (11:1 on espresso) |
| honey | `#D2A26B` | "a beautiful honey blonde 🍯" — buttons and highlights on dark grounds |
| espresso / umber | `#1F1612` / `#2E211B` | the dark hair she specialises in — dark chapters |
| mauve | `#B9A3B5` | the heart from her logo, muted — decorative only |
| cream / sand / latte | `#F7F1EA` / `#EEE4D8` / `#EFE6DF` | grounds |
| ink / mocha | `#231915` / `#6B574B` | text |

- **Type:** Fraunces (soft, warm serif, `SOFT` 60) for headings; Jost (a
  Futura-like sans, close to her logo's "HAIR BY") for text and labels;
  Allura for the script "Oshi" — the closest open font to her logo.
- **Shape:** the capsule — a lock of hair, her arched mirror, the arched door
  of the suite. Photos sit in capsules and arches.
- **Motion:** light moving across dark hair — the hero capsule holds Oshi
  herself and, as it opens to full screen, she dissolves into her glossy
  dark-hair work; strands of light draw themselves across chapter I; a
  single strand down the left edge tracks the journey; nano-particles travel
  into a strand's layers.
