# The photo plan — which posts to pull, and where each one goes

**How to read this.** Instagram blocks logged-out access, so I could not open
these posts or see the images. What I *could* read was the caption text that
search engines have indexed. So: the captions below are real and quoted, and
the post URLs are real — but **the picture is my inference from the caption.**
Open each one, check it matches, and swap in something better if it doesn't.
You know her grid; I'm working from text.

Confidence is marked. **High** = the snippet explicitly attributed the caption
to `hair_by_oshi_` with a date and like count. **Medium** = it carried her
profile and her Oakleigh South location tag.

## Getting them out

Fastest for the whole grid at original quality — Instagram's own export:

**Profile → ☰ → Accounts Centre → Your information and permissions →
Download your information → her account → Some of your information →
tick *Posts* → Download to device → All time → High quality → Submit**

Arrives by email in ~10 minutes. Photos are in `media/posts/`.

For single posts it's faster to just open each one on her phone and screenshot,
or pull the originals from her camera roll — those are better than anything
Instagram will give back, since it re-compresses on upload.

**Export settings:** JPEG, sRGB, under ~400 KB each. Big files are the quickest
way to make a good site feel cheap.
