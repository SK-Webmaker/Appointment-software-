Dark hair is not the absence of colour. It is depth, weight and the way light travels through it. Lifting it is easy. Keeping it healthy while you do is the entire skill.

03

Keratin

Smoothing · Protein · Manageability

Keratin deposits protein into the hair to smooth and straighten. It is not permanent and it does not change your curl pattern forever — anyone telling you otherwise is selling something. Done right, it buys months of easy mornings.

  • Best for frizz-prone, humid-reactive hair
  • Straighter finish than nanoplasty
  • Wears off gradually and evenly
  • Paired with the right home care

Pricing depends on length, density and condition — which is why it's quoted in your consult, not guessed on a website.

03 — The signature

Nanoplasty, actually explained

What it is

A formaldehyde-free smoothing treatment. Low-molecular amino acids slip into the cuticle and seal it flat, so light bounces off one smooth surface instead of scattering off a raised one. That is the shine.

What it is not

Not a permanent straightener, not a chemical relaxer. It will not destroy your curl pattern. It softens and relaxes — it does not erase.

Versus keratin

Keratin gives a straighter, more uniform finish. Nanoplasty keeps more of your natural movement and is gentler on coloured hair. It depends whether you want straight or easy.

Who it's for

Dense, coarse, frizz-prone hair that takes forever to dry. If your blow-dry is a 45-minute event and Melbourne humidity undoes it in ten, this is the one.

10 — Before you book

The honest answers

How much will my appointment cost?

It depends on length, density and what your hair has been through. That's not a dodge — quoting a flat price for hair this variable is how people end up with surprise bills. Send a photo through the free consult and you'll get a real number before you commit.

Nanoplasty or keratin — which one?

Want it genuinely straighter and more uniform? Keratin. Want to keep your movement and just lose the frizz and the drying time? Nanoplasty. If your hair is already coloured, nanoplasty is usually the gentler choice.

Will a smoothing treatment ruin my curls?

No. Neither treatment is a permanent relaxer. They soften and relax the cuticle, and they wear off gradually. Your curl pattern comes back.

I've used box dye. Can you fix it?

Usually, yes — but it takes time. Box dye deposits unpredictable pigment that has to be lifted evenly without wrecking the hair. Colour correction is often more expensive and slower than maintaining colour properly, and it's frequently more than one appointment. Better to know that up front.

I'm going grey. Do I need permanent colour?

If you're under about 40% grey, permanent colour is usually overkill — it commits you to a hard regrowth line forever. A blend or a demi-permanent gloss looks softer and grows out far more kindly.
