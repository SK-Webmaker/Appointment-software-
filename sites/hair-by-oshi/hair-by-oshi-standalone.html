01 — The craft

Dark hair is not the absence of colour. It is depth, weight and the way light travels through it. Lifting it is easy. Keeping it healthy while you do is the entire skill.

Nanoplasty Signature

Formaldehyde-free · Frizz-free · Keeps movement

The service Oshi is known for across Melbourne. Nanoplasty uses low-molecular amino acids to smooth the cuticle without flattening your hair into a sheet. You keep your body and your bend — you lose the frizz and the fight.

  • Safe on coloured and previously treated hair
  • Cuts blow-dry time dramatically
  • Softens bulk in dense, coarse hair
  • Grows out soft — no harsh regrowth line
03

Keratin

Smoothing · Protein · Manageability

Keratin deposits protein into the hair to smooth and straighten. It is not permanent and it does not change your curl pattern forever — anyone telling you otherwise is selling something. Done right, it buys you months of easy mornings.

  • Best for frizz-prone, humidity-reactive hair
  • Straighter finish than nanoplasty
  • Wears off gradually and evenly
  • Paired with the right home care, or it is wasted

Every service starts with a consult. Pricing depends on length, density and condition — which is why it is quoted in your DM, not guessed on a website.

03 — The signature

Nanoplasty, actually explained

What it is

A formaldehyde-free smoothing treatment. Low-molecular amino acids slip into the cuticle and seal it flat, so light bounces off one smooth surface instead of scattering off a raised one. That is the shine.

What it is not

It is not a permanent straightener and it is not a chemical relaxer. It will not destroy your curl pattern. It softens and relaxes — it does not erase.

Nanoplasty vs keratin

Keratin gives a straighter, more uniform finish. Nanoplasty keeps more of your natural movement and is gentler on already-coloured hair. Which one you want depends on whether you want straight or easy.

Who it is for

Dense, coarse, frizz-prone hair that takes forever to dry. If your blow-dry is a 45-minute event and Melbourne humidity undoes it in ten minutes, this is the one.

08 — Before you book

The honest answers

How much will my appointment cost?

It depends on length, density and what your hair has been through. That's not a dodge — quoting a flat price for hair this variable is how people end up with surprise bills. Send a photo in a DM and you'll get a real number before you commit.

Nanoplasty or keratin — which one?

Want it genuinely straighter and more uniform? Keratin. Want to keep your movement and just lose the frizz and the drying time? Nanoplasty. If your hair is already coloured, nanoplasty is usually the gentler choice.

Will a smoothing treatment ruin my curls?

No. Neither treatment is a permanent relaxer. They soften and relax the cuticle, and they wear off gradually. Your curl pattern comes back.

I've used box dye. Can you fix it?

Usually, yes — but it takes time. Box dye deposits unpredictable pigment that has to be lifted evenly without wrecking the hair. Colour correction is often more expensive and slower than maintaining colour properly, and it's frequently more than one appointment. Better to know that up front.

I'm going grey. Do I need permanent colour?

If you're under about 40% grey, permanent colour is usually overkill — it commits you to a hard regrowth line forever. A blend or a demi-permanent gloss looks softer and grows out far more kindly.
